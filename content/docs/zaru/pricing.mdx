# Pricing & Plans

Zaru offers four tiers. All tiers include access to the full chat interface, agent execution,
and the Glass Laboratory. Higher tiers unlock additional capacity, team features, and
deployment options.

---

## What Each Tier Includes

### Free

Get started at no cost. Includes the full chat interface, all conversation modes, and
single-shot code execution. Limited to 1 concurrent agent execution, a 5-minute execution
timeout, and limited persistent storage. Rate limits: 3 agent executions per minute, 20
per day.

### Pro

Everything in Free, plus higher capacity for serious individual use. 5 concurrent executions,
30-minute timeout, 10 GB persistent storage, and BYOK model support. Rate limits: 10 agent
executions per minute, 200 per day. BYOK users are exempt from LLM-specific rate limits.

### Business

Everything in Pro, plus team and organization features. Per-seat pricing for your team, 50+
concurrent executions, 4-hour timeout, 50 GB storage, custom agent and workflow manifests,
audit logs with 90-day retention, and priority support. Rate limits: 30 agent executions
per minute, 1,000 per day. Limits are customizable per tenant.

### Enterprise

Everything in Business, plus self-hosted and air-gapped deployment options, SAML SSO
federation, 100 GB storage, 24-hour timeout, unlimited concurrent executions, and audit logs
with 365-day retention. Rate limits: 100 agent executions per minute, 10,000 per day. All
limits are fully customizable per tenant and per user.

## Billing

### Monthly vs Annual

Both monthly and annual billing cycles are available. Annual billing offers a discount
compared to monthly pricing.

### Per-Seat Pricing

Business and Enterprise tiers are billed per seat. Each member in your organization counts
as one seat. Adding or removing members adjusts the next invoice automatically through
prorated billing.

### How to Upgrade

Upgrade from the `/pricing` page in the Zaru client or from the **Upgrade** button in
[The Colony](/docs/zaru/the-colony) billing tab. The upgrade flow redirects to Stripe's
hosted checkout for secure payment processing. Your tier updates automatically once payment
succeeds.

### Managing Billing

Click **Manage Billing** in the Colony billing tab to open the Stripe Customer Portal. From
there you can update payment methods, download invoices, switch between monthly and annual
billing, and cancel your subscription.

---

## Self-Hosted Deployments

Self-hosted and air-gapped deployments (Enterprise tier) do not use Stripe. Billing is
disabled entirely — tier assignment is managed directly through the platform's identity
provider or admin API. All billing-related endpoints return `501 Not Implemented` when
Stripe is not configured.
